Privacy Policy – HomeBox

Last updated: August 2026

HomeBox stores your home management data locally on your device. We do not operate a user account server or collect sensitive personal information.

Data We Store

  • Bills, warranties, repair costs
  • PDF guide file paths
  • App preferences and shop unlocks

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store payment card information.
